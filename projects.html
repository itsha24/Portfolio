AI Job Matcher & Client Finder

Runs twice a day on GitHub Actions: pulls fresh developer postings from company career pages and Adzuna, filters out senior roles and internships for free, reads each full posting, and has an LLM score how well it fits my profile as structured JSON (Gemini or Claude). Strong fresh matches are emailed to me, and everything lands on a live dashboard ranked by fit and freshness.

Every week it also finds local businesses on Google Maps, audits their websites, and drafts bilingual outreach for my freelance work. In its first live run, 70 of 97 new postings were filtered without AI and only 25 needed scoring.
